Fair play

Rules & Regulations

Six ground rules every team must follow. Read them once, break none of them.

01
Team size

Minimum 1, maximum 4 members per team.

02
Team composition

No combined-department teams. No mixed-gender teams. All members must be from the same department and same gender group.

03
Coding requirement

Traditional coding is not required. Any AI tool is allowed, but AI-generated content must be modified and adapted by your team — not used as-is.

04
Time discipline

Start and submission times are strictly enforced. No extensions after the deadline.

05
Attendance

If a team member needs to leave or is unreachable during the event, it must be reported to their respective department immediately.

06
Eligibility

Open to all students. Only one team is allowed to participate per class.

Breaking any rule may lead to instant disqualification
